The Spa and Salon Industry in the Arab World 2026-2027: Trends That Will Change the Rules

Published: May 18, 2026 Author: Zanketsu Team

The spa and beauty salon industry in the Arab world is undergoing a massive transformation. The modern salon client is no longer looking for just a quick haircut or manicure; they seek a premium, personalized, and digital-first experience.

Salons that rely on traditional walk-ins and phone calls are losing market share to modern facilities that leverage technology. Let's analyze the 5 major trends redefining salons and spas in 2026-2027.

Trend 1: Smart Bookings Ends the "Phone-Only" Era

Clients often decide to book beauty sessions late at night when the salon is closed. Expecting them to call between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM means missing out on bookings.

Providing a 24/7 online booking portal integrated with Apple Pay and local credit cards allows clients to book instantly. This reduces receptionist workloads and increases bookings by up to 35%.

Trend 2: Monthly Subscriptions & Memberships

Relying on single pay-per-visit bookings makes cash flow unpredictable. Modern salons are adopting subscription models (e.g., 4 blow-drys and 1 hair treatment per month for a fixed fee).

This locks in recurring revenue and turns casual visitors into highly loyal clients, stabilizing the salon's cash flow.

Trend 3: Client History & Personalized Profiles

When a client visits a salon, they expect the stylist to know their preferred hair color formula, their tea/coffee choice, and their styling history.

A dedicated salon CRM stores these preferences securely. When a client books, the stylist reviews their history, ensuring a personalized experience every time.

Trend 4: Backbar Product Inventory Audits

Shampoos, conditioners, and hair dyes are highly vulnerable to wastage and theft if left unmonitored.

Cloud software auto-deducts the precise amount of backbar product used for each service logged (e.g. 50g of hair dye). This maintains inventory accuracy and safeguards salon profits.

Trend 5: Complete End-to-End Digital Experience

From automated SMS booking confirmations and reminders to digital invoicing and post-service review emails, the client journey must be digital. This establishes a highly professional brand image.
